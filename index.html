Description

A residence of great character in a private setting close to Inniskeen/Carrickmacross and just 15 minutes from the M1 motorway. The property is easily accessible to Dundalk, Newry, the Cooley Peninsula and Slieve Gullion Forest Park. Approached through electric gates, this light-filled and spacious residence overlooks beautifully maintained gardens with an abundance of colour. The gardens include an attractive garden house, raised patio with outdoor barbecue kitchen and raised children`s playhouse, making it an ideal setting for entertaining and family life. The property has many fine features throughout, including superior kitchen and bathroom fittings, high-quality floor coverings, curtains and light fittings.

Location

It is 3km from Inniskeen, 10km from Carrickmacross, 15 km from Dundalk and Dublin airport is less than one hour away. Inniskeen is a quaint village steeped in history and is synonymous with one of Ireland`s greatest poets, Patrick Kavanagh. The village offers a good range of facilities including national school, shops, pubs, Raglan Road Cafe, regular bus service to Dundalk/Carrickmacross and many leisure and sporting amenities.

Accommodation

Entrance hallway

With parquet style ceramic tiled floor, ceiling coving

Living Room - 7m (23'0") x 4.5m (14'9")

A superb open plan living and entertaining space filled with natural light. Features include a ceramic tiled floor, fireplace with raised hearth and cut-stone surround incorporating a Hamco stove, built in cabinetry, ceiling coving and recessed lighting.

The bespoke cream fitted kitchen by SLEEK features an outstanding range of floor and wall units with granite worktops, Siemens double oven, Bora induction hob with downdraft extractor, integrated fridge/freezer and dishwasher. A built-in dresser/drinks cabinet features attractive backlighting and a marble countertop. Sliding doors open directly onto the patio area, creating an excellent connection between the indoor and outdoor living spaces.

Boot Room

Practical boot room with an extensive range of built-in shelving, tiled flooring and direct access to the rear garden.

Bedroom 1 - 7.3m (23'11") x 4.6m (15'1")

A generously proportioned master bedroom featuring a brick surround mock fireplace with electric flame fire and mounted TV stand. Rustic oak beams add warmth and character, while electric blinds to the Velux roof windows provide excellent convenience. The bedroom also benefits from a walk in wardrobe area with fitted wardrobes, sliderobes and drawers.

En Suite

Fully tiled shower room with rain power shower, WC and WHB.

Bedroom 2 - 3.8m (12'6") x 3.2m (10'6")

Bedroom 3 - 3.6m (11'10") x 2.6m (8'6")

Bedroom 4 - 3.7m (12'2") x 1.7m (5'7")

Flexible additional room suitable for use as a fourth bedroom, home office or study, with fitted wardrobes and shelving.

Bathroom

Beautifully appointed bathroom featuring a vintage style bath on castors, spacious rain power shower, WC, wash hand basin and vanity unit. The room combines tiled flooring with part timber panelled walls and is finished with an elegant chandelier.

Utility Room

Well equipped utility room with an extensive range of fitted units and shelving, stainless steel sink unit and Danfoss air to water heating unit. Access to the attic is provided via a Stira.
